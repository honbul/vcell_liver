Demonstration scripts contain other epoch counts. The exact reference values and public run IDs are in [the reference TSV](../manifests/reference/gears_fig2f_author.tsv). scGPT: Fig. 3a is driven by `tutorials/Fig3_AB_PerturbPred.py` in the manuscript release. Its [Figshare data index](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/scGPT_reproducibility/24954519) points to the public GEARS Norman archive. The [environment freezes](../manifests/environment) document the new runtime. Both runners add epoch checkpoints and local output; the paper tracks retain native selection and evaluator logic. The differences in runtime and available assets make both runs modified reproductions.

## Execution and verification

All five GEARS replays and the scGPT replay completed on G1: GEARS seed 1 `17663`, GEARS seeds 2–5 `17665`, and scGPT `17664`. The strict handoff `17672` accepted all six results after verifying execution logs and hashes for splits, predictions and model checkpoints. The [submission manifest](../manifests/slurm/study_submission.tsv) records their dependencies.

The [split metadata check](preflight/paper_split_alignment.json) records the split hashes and matches the four reported subgroup counts to the original no-perturb GEARS logs for all five seeds and to the linked scGPT run. scGPT has 9/52/18/37 conditions in `combo_seen0`/`combo_seen1`/`combo_seen2`/`unseen_single`. Across all six regenerated splits, treating reversed target order as the same intervention produced no cross-role overlap. These checks do not establish the identity of the original private cells or control pairs.

## GEARS: origin of the numerical difference

The figure denominator was verified from all five original `No_Perturb` run sources and output logs. The pinned historical model forwards each input control cell's expression directly, and its training script bypasses optimization. R1 uses the same identity operation on the newly generated pairs. [Baseline provenance and subgroup counts](../manifests/reference/gears_fig2f_no_perturb_provenance.json) preserve the original logs' hashes.

The GEARS seed 1 [execution audit](paper/R1-s1/execution_audit.json) confirms all 20 epochs, the final marker, test condition coverage and the recorded hashes for the split, prediction and model files. The [same-seed comparison](paper/R1-s1/same_seed_comparison.tsv) gives normalized error of 0.22839 / 0.33268 / 0.42942, versus the author runs' seed 1 values of 0.25916 / 0.31056 / 0.29896 for 0/1/2 unseen targets. The largest difference is in the nine two-unseen-target conditions: the GEARS numerator rises from 0.15578 to 0.22364, while the no-perturb denominator is close (0.52107 versus 0.52079).

The published grouping is assigned by the outer simulation split before the [pinned splitter](https://github.com/snap-stanford/GEARS/blob/9d27bd619935cebe6f1519c3b53f61d2777dee59/gears/data_utils.py) carves out validation. For this replay the final train singles alone differ from the labels in 4/8/0 conditions for 0/1/2 unseen targets. Counting train plus validation singles resolves all 12 differences. The comparison preserves the published grouping.

Seed 1 diagnostic: the author used a private preprocessed dataset and saved pairs, so exact cell, gene and pair identity cannot be checked. Both scripts initialize a fresh GEARS model and train for 20 epochs with batch size 32. The replay records 1,602 batches per epoch. Both logs print through step 1,601 every epoch, consistent with 1,601–1,650 author batches under the pinned code's 50-step logging cadence. The minimum logged validation top-20 DE MSE occurs at epoch 3 for the author (0.1561) and epoch 6 for the replay (0.1441, both rounded); the best checkpoint thus differs before test scoring. The [author run](https://wandb.ai/kexinhuang/pert_gnn_simulation_norman2019/runs/3m9uygq6) package record lists Torch 1.12.1+cu116 and PyG 2.1.0 (requirements SHA-256 `10ee0e5e62a05cc64140950ec39500ea24c8a4ac602642b14971012388010079`); this replay uses Torch 2.5.1+cu124 and PyG 2.6.1. The test table uses the pinned native non-dropout evaluator. The observed discrepancy cannot be attributed to any one of the data, pairing or runtime differences.

The independent seed 1 spot check (Slurm `17702`, exit 0) confirmed condition coverage and counts, gene order, and measured condition means against the public matrix. Recomputing top-20 non-dropout MSE from saved predictions agreed exactly with all 116 condition values in the GEARS column (maximum absolute difference 0). This checks the local metric calculation; the full paper audit still covers all repetitions.

The [seed 2](paper/R1-s2/same_seed_comparison.tsv), [seed 3](paper/R1-s3/same_seed_comparison.tsv), [seed 4](paper/R1-s4/same_seed_comparison.tsv) and [seed 5](paper/R1-s5/same_seed_comparison.tsv) replays also completed all 20 epochs and passed the same artifact and log audit. Seeds 3–5 have higher normalized errors than their matched author runs in all three groups. For two unseen targets, seed 3 has only two conditions and scores 0.43498 versus 0.42145; seed 4 has six conditions and scores 0.39950 versus 0.38782; seed 5 has ten conditions and scores 0.52541 versus 0.49551.

The [five-split GEARS comparison](paper/gears_fig2f_five_splits.tsv) reports normalized MSE of 0.24373 / 0.34879 / 0.44731, compared with the authors’ 0.23215 / 0.33218 / 0.40935 for 0/1/2 unseen targets. The score increases with the number of unseen targets in both runs, and the replay has higher error in all three groups. For two unseen targets, the unnormalized GEARS MSE is 0.31519 versus 0.28901, while the no-perturb denominator is 0.70465 versus 0.70603. The gap is primarily in the numerator. This is a completed modified reproduction with a numerical discrepancy; exact reproduction cannot be verified with the public assets.

## scGPT: origin of the numerical difference

The [scGPT replay](paper/R2/result.json) ran without the original preprocessing cache or paired controls. The [public checkpoint load check](preflight/scgpt_paper_checkpoint_load.json) verified 153 backbone tensors by value and confirmed that the key set matches the author log after QKV name translation; the original tensor values were unavailable. The vocabulary matched 4,547 of 5,045 measured genes. Both runs used 15 epochs and 686 batches per epoch. The validation Pearson selected epoch 15 in the author log (0.99048831) and epoch 13 in the replay (0.99051053). The original used FlashAttention; the replay used PyTorch attention. The native 1,536-gene training sample retained 245,861 of 809,590 target flags in the replay (30.37%); the author log does not provide a matching count. The flag omission is measured in the replay, but its contribution to the score gap is unknown. The common benchmark uses a separate sampler that keeps all targets.

## Independent metric check

The [independent evaluator check](paper/paper_diagnostics.json) completed successfully (Slurm `17680`). It verified the saved output panel and measured condition means. It reproduced the GEARS non-dropout MSE exactly on all 560 condition-by-split rows and the scGPT author-code metrics on all 116 conditions. Replacing the scGPT DE set with the top 20 non-dropout genes gives a diagnostic ΔPearson of 0.75110. That sensitivity to the gene set does not identify how the publisher bar was calculated, so 0.72074 remains the replay's primary DE ΔPearson. The original private assets prevent separating data, control-pairing and runtime effects.
