# Manuscript claims and supporting evidence

| Claim | Status | Evidence and scope |
|---|---|---|
| Shared vehicle means changed validation and gain selection | Development evidence | Matched physical control count, independent roles and paired comparisons on the existing GSE92742 development support. [D1 and D2](../D12_RESULTS.md) |
| Earlier same-drug transcriptional measurements improved later HEPG2 prediction | Prespecified within-assay test passed | Exact exposure, held canonical parent and independent later plate, 132 compounds. The matched-dose wrong-parent and fit-mean controls were added after the original T4 result. [T4](../T4_RESULTS.md) · [Attribution](../T4_ATTRIBUTION_RESULTS.md) |
| The same-assay HEPG2 temporal gain persists after exact scaffold blocking | Registered development follow-up passed | On 132 held parents, the rank-16 operator reduced RMSE by 7.24% and increased pooled R² by 14.27 points over a scalar using identical scaffold-purged fitting data. Both metrics supported the effect in a scaffold-cluster sensitivity. [Scaffold-purged transfer](../T4_SCAFFOLD_RESULTS.md) |
| The rank-16 operator outperformed a State adaptation given the same early measurements | Registered development follow-up passed | On the same 132 HEPG2 parents, RMSE was 7.44% lower and pooled R² 15.27 points higher than a one-token State Transition refit with identical 6-hour inputs and scaffold-purged fit conditions. [Matched State comparison](../T4_STATE_RESULTS.md) |
| The temporal signal survived a change in RNA well index and distinguished compounds within plate and dose | Registered follow-up and frozen rescoring | On 59 parents with different-position early measurements, RMSE was 6.82% below the matched scalar. The scalar led on the 35-parent matched-donor-count subset. Centered contrasts favored the operator over State and the scalar on 69 parents, while cosine Top-1 favored the scalar. [Position and retrieval](../T4_POSITION_RESULTS.md) |
| The temporal model was superior to a directly measured different-plate 24 h replicate | Calibrated repeat outperformed the 6 h model | The original unscaled comparison was unresolved in HEPG2. Fitting a scalar on different 24 h plates from fitting parents yielded a better 24 h replicate comparator in both HEPG2 and PHH. [Calibrated repeat](../T4_REPEAT_RESULTS.md) |
| Standard R17 residual blend improved over a matched nonhepatic donor anchor | Development evidence | 493 parents and 2,324 HEPG2 wells. Same measured donor inputs across the comparison. Structural features in its neural branch had no demonstrated isolated advantage over shuffle. [S/L B](../SL_B_RESULTS.md) |
| Independently measured basal RNA improved context conditioning with the same measured RNA donors | Gate unmet | The matched B comparison showed higher RMSE and lower pooled R² for basal RNA than identity/OOV on five held-cell contexts. [B context](../XB_RESULTS.md) |
| A gene-wise vehicle variance correction improved over a scalar across L1000 contexts | Practical gate unmet | The structure-only follow-up showed small harm relative to a scalar. A separate donor-informed comparison yielded a gain below the registered practical threshold. [A calibration](../TA_RESULTS.md) · [B calibration](../T123_RESULTS.md) |
| Changing the head, loss or phenotype features delivered a practical performance gain | Gate unmet | Canonical-parent-paired A and B ablations and missing-view fallback tests. Low-rank heads reduced operations in S/L. The I5 kernel effect was a small replay gain. [S/L A](../SL_RESULTS.md) · [S/L B](../SL_B_RESULTS.md) · [I5](../I5_RESULTS.md) |
| The within-LINCS temporal effect generalized across assay or cell types | Unresolved | Frozen directions had mean cosine from −0.017 to +0.024 across the two previously exposed exact-condition HepaRG sets, with only 3 and 5 parents. Both cellular context and assay changed. [HepaRG](../T4_CROSS_ASSAY_RESULTS.md) |
| Available LINCS releases support an adult HCC temporal comparison under the reserved data contract | Insufficient support | GSE92742 HUH7 had 94 exact-dose, repeated parents before exclusions. All six supporting RNA plates lost vehicle support with reservations enforced, and no CMap pair survived GEO alias exclusion. The audit read metadata only. [Adult HCC temporal support](../HCC_TIME_LINCS_RESULTS.md) |
| A learned same-time transfer residual improves JHH-5 prediction beyond the same measured source-drug input | Gate unmet | On 609 JHH-5 parents at exactly 10 µM, same-assay rank-16 residuals increased RMSE by 2.07% versus an identically fitted affine anchor and were indistinguishable from a residual parent shuffle. Simple donor-based comparators gave the best point estimates. The target measurements had been included in B1. [JHH-5 transfer](../HCC_LINCS_24H_TRANSFER_RESULTS.md) |
| The identity of a same-dose, same-time nonhepatic donor contributed to JHH-5 prediction | Registered development attribution | The original donor mean reduced RMSE by 4.04% versus the equal-input State adaptation and by 7.60% versus same-plate wrong-parent inputs on the 609-parent B1-exposed set. [JHH-5 attribution](../JHH5_DONOR_ATTRIBUTION_RESULTS.md) |
| Gene-wise shrinkage of the nonhepatic donor consensus improved JHH-5 vector prediction | Registered development criteria passed | Without JHH-5 fit labels, relative RMSE improved by 0.645% and pooled R² by 0.953 points versus the direct donor mean. Gains were also observed against the target-fit origin scalar. All but four compounds had at least 16 donor cell lines. [JHH-5 shrinkage](../JHH5_DONOR_ATTRIBUTION_RESULTS.md) |
| The donor mean transferred to a second adult HCC line | Single-plate technical support | In JHH-7, the mean reduced RMSE by 13.71% versus State trained only on 475 scaffold-purged JHH-5 parents. The 99 scored parents were in B1, and 95 appeared in the JHH-5 development cohort. The fixed shrinkage gain did not meet the technical threshold. [JHH-7 transfer](../JHH7_DONOR_TRANSFER_RESULTS.md) |
| The 16-line donor response can be retained with fewer measured contexts | Noninferiority gate unmet | With matched supports and nested cell draws, the eight-line average increased RMSE relative to the 16-line average by about 3% in both JHH lines. Eight-line shrinkage improved over B0 on pooled comparisons and had higher errors than 16-line shrinkage. [Donor panel size](../HCC_DONOR_PANEL_SIZE_RESULTS.md) |
| Eight-donor shrinkage improved on an equally informed State adaptation | Development support under matched inputs | RMSE decreased by 2.31% in JHH-5 and 10.20% in the single-plate JHH-7 comparison. The JHH-5 joint gate also required an improvement against the same-input scalar. Its RMSE gain was 0.194% with a 99% interval spanning zero. [Matched eight-donor transfer](../HCC_DONOR8_STATE_RESULTS.md) |
| The eight-donor advantage extends to JHH-5 at 3.33333 µM | Gate unmet on previously unscored treatment wells | On 490 parents at 24 h, the State RMSE comparison included zero. The fitted scalar and the zero-response reference had lower RMSE and higher pooled R² than shrinkage in all three folds. The 10 and 3.33333 µM analyses share target plates and vehicles. [Low-dose comparison](../JHH_LOW_DOSE_TRANSFER_RESULTS.md) |
| Adding same-compound 10 µM measurements improves the 1.11111 µM JHH-5 prediction | Gate unmet against the same-dose scalar | On 467 parents, the two-dose combination reduced RMSE by 2.029% relative to the matched State adaptation. Gain versus the low-dose-only scalar was −0.013% (99% interval −0.069 to 0.028%). The target cohorts share plates and vehicles with previously studied doses. [Dose bridge](../JHH_DOSE_BRIDGE_RESULTS.md) |
| Low-dose calibration from JHH-5 can predict responses in JHH-7 | Registered single-plate technical criteria passed at 1 µM | On 101 JHH-7 parents, the JHH-5-only scalar reduced RMSE by 6.10% against same-input State, 2.68% against no response and 3.59% against a gain-matched wrong-compound donor. The intervals against the raw and shrinkage means encompassed zero. The 0.1 µM comparison is descriptive. [JHH-7 low-dose transfer](../JHH7_LOW_DOSE_RESULTS.md) |
| The same-compound signal is detectable after separating compound and RNA well index | Registered post hoc evidence on one target plate | At 1 µM on 80 JHH-7 parents, same-compound U2OS donors at different well indices reduced RMSE by 3.14% (99% interval 0.39–5.89%) relative to other-compound donors at the target index on the same source plates. The single-cell-line donor was worse than B0. Only one target had at least eight off-position donor cell lines. [Position attribution](../JHH7_POSITION_ATTRIBUTION_RESULTS.md) |
| Calibrated shrinkage improves on alternative models given eight off-position donor measurements | Registered development criteria passed on B1-exposed treatments | On 305 JHH-5 parents, RMSE was 0.751% lower than the fitted scalar and 3.402% lower than State receiving the eight individual donor vectors. Both metrics passed against the scalar, State, wrong-drug and zero-response comparisons. Removing the ASG002 plate left an interval spanning zero against zero response. [Off-position eight-source comparison](../JHH5_OFF_POSITION_RESULTS.md) |
| The off-position gain persists against five public adaptations on six JHH-5 plates | Registered post hoc criteria passed | On 305 B1-exposed parents, RMSE was 0.751% lower and pooled R² 1.662 points higher than the resampled best public comparator. Both interval directions persisted under every single-plate deletion. The best adapters reduced to the fitted donor scalar. [Expanded six-plate comparison](../JHH5_OFF_POSITION_NATIVE_RESULTS.md) |
| The off-position 10 µM model transfers to JHH-7 | All four registered single-plate criteria passed | On 88 B1-exposed JHH-7 parents, calibrated shrinkage reduced RMSE by 1.278% versus the fitted scalar and by 8.195% versus State. Both comparators were fitted with 203 JHH-5 parents after excluding the full JHH-7 target parents and scaffolds. Comparison with uncalibrated shrinkage included zero in both intervals. [Cross-cell position-disjoint transfer](../JHH7_OFF_POSITION_RESULTS.md) |
| The single-plate gain persists against five public VC adaptations | Registered post hoc comparison passed | On the same 88 JHH-7 parents, calibrated shrinkage reduced RMSE by 1.278% versus the resampled best VC and increased pooled R² by 2.945 points. PRnet, chemCPA and TranSiGen selected zero residual gain in each rotation and tied their donor scalar. [Expanded public comparator set](../JHH7_OFF_POSITION_NATIVE_RESULTS.md) |
| The gains remain under scaffold clustering and are concentrated among high-influence compounds | Frozen post hoc sensitivity | Both improvement directions persisted after scaffold resampling in both cohorts. Removing the 20 largest squared-error contributors reduced the best-public-adapter RMSE gains to 0.461% in JHH-5 and 0.218% in JHH-7. [Contribution and clustering](../JHH_OFF_POSITION_ROBUSTNESS_RESULTS.md) |
| Gene-specific donor variance adds signal beyond the matched prediction norm | Registered post hoc attribution, small effect | Relative RMSE gains against amplitude-bin variance permutations at the same norm were 0.110% and 0.144% in JHH-5 and JHH-7. Both metrics supported the direction at 99%, below the practical point thresholds. [Variance controls](../JHH_DONOR_VARIANCE_RESULTS.md) |
| A temporal correction also helped within primary human hepatocytes | Limited to scalar versus zero response | On 67 held parents, a fit-only scalar improved over B0. A fixed rank-16 operator did not improve both endpoints over the scalar. The operators were fitted and scored within PHH. [PHH](../T4_PHH_RESULTS.md) |
| A HEPG2-fitted temporal operator improves the prediction of PHH responses in the same assay | Gate unmet | On 67 held PHH parents, the operator increased RMSE by 4.54% versus a HEPG2-fitted scalar and by 6.18% versus a PHH-fitted scalar given identical PHH 6 h measurements. Both R² intervals favored the scalar. [Context transfer](../T4_CONTEXT_TRANSFER_RESULTS.md) |
| The method outperforms existing virtual cells in adult HCC | Unverified | A matched external comparison with adult HCC and predeclared benchmarks is needed. R18 C1 was evaluated under R16 before reservation. R18 C3 is a small HUH7 technical support set. R18 itself currently has metadata, synthetic processing tests and structure inputs. [External confirmation scope](../R18_CONFIRMATION_PROTOCOL.md) |
| SNU-423 high-dose analogue copying demonstrated a broad chemical advantage | Unverified | Six of 203 parents supplied 90.8% of positive SSE savings. The original R² point of +8.11 points versus the best comparator had a scaffold-component sensitivity interval of −0.64 to +15.37 points. [Tahoe scaffold sensitivity](../HCC_SCAFFOLD_RESULTS.md) |
| Fixing the within-well cell-line panel yields an adult HCC improvement over State | Gate unmet | The common 44-line endpoint eliminated vehicle-role differences to numerical precision. On 203 scaffold-purged parents, hybrid ECFP and sibling copying increased RMSE by 0.66% relative to fresh matched State and by 0.67% relative to zero response. [Fixed-panel refit](../HCC_FIXED_PANEL_REFIT_RESULTS.md) |
| Sharing responses from the other 43 lines improves the fixed-panel SNU-423 endpoint | Gate unmet | With the held chemical removed from all lines, the separable kernel worsened both metrics against single-line ECFP, State and both auxiliary-label controls under scaffold purging. [Multitask kernel](../HCC_FIXED44_MULTITASK_RESULTS.md) |
| External phenotypes rescued scaffold-blocked SNU-423 | Gate unmet | On 119 jointly profiled parents and all 203 with fallback, Cell Painting and nonhepatic PRISM did not jointly improve RMSE and pooled R² over ECFP or zero. Mask and parent-shuffle intervals included zero. [SNU-423 phenotype inputs](../HCC_PHENOTYPE_RESULTS.md) |
| Same-drug nonhepatic RNA rescued structurally distant SNU-423 prediction | Gate unmet | The fixed rank-4 map increased RMSE against the matched scalar and RNA-augmented State on 94 held parents with Tanimoto below 0.7. Most source measurements were 10 µM and all target measurements were 5 µM. [External donor analysis](../SNU423_DONOR_RESULTS.md) |
| The Tahoe SNU-423 repeats establish drug-response reliability across well positions | Insufficient support | The exact-entity comparison had two different-position parents per dose and 50 same-position parents at 5 µM. No target well had both donor types. [Repeat analysis](../TAHOE_HCC_REPEAT_RESULTS.md) |
| Fitting three doses improved prediction for new SNU-423 compounds at 5 µM | Gate unmet | The joint-dose ECFP kernel beat the matched ChemBERTa kernel. | HepaRG high-dose responses contain reproducible variation beyond a position-context anchor | Registered development follow-up | On 281 GSE296254 parents, frozen structure and dose reduced RMSE by 0.77%, while different-culture and different-position repeats reduced RMSE by 5.90% on repeat-supported wells. The public human HCC studies examined in a targeted follow-up primarily support one- or two-compound comparisons. The audit separates registered sequencing entries from source specimens and records inconsistent sample assignments. [Public HCC support audit](../HCC_PUBLIC_SUPPORT.md)

Across the expanded metadata review, three independent-source HUH7 comparisons had documented 5 µM and 24 h exposures. Two GEO series had dose discrepancies with the original article. The design metadata remained insufficient for a common independent-coordinate benchmark. [External HCC preflight](../HCC_EXTERNAL_PREFLIGHT.md)

The final manuscript requires separate evidence for the adult HCC claim, documented data and code access, and author-confirmed contributions and declarations. The development draft can be read alongside the outcome files. [Submission guidance](https://www.nature.com/srep/author-instructions/submission-guidelines)

## Regenerate the figures

Use Python with Matplotlib and the repository's PyYAML dependency. The script checks the score table hashes against their receipts and uses the recorded relative RMSE and cosine values.

```bash
python -m src.r19_plan.t4_evidence_figure \
  --data reports/r19_plan/data \
  --output reports/r19_plan/manuscript/figure1.pdf

python -m src.r19_plan.hcc_evidence_figure \
  --data reports/r19_plan/data \
  --output reports/r19_plan/manuscript/figure2.pdf

python -m src.r19_plan.jhh_off_position_figure \
  --data reports/r19_plan/data \
  --output reports/r19_plan/manuscript/figure3.pdf
```
