# H1 plan lock

Base commit `5ec8ed231ee88c6ae578d0e1cb30d7591c792392`. Lock follows the identity-only coverage and hash audit. H1 tries to improve ranking of unmeasured compounds using measured responses to a prespecified anchor panel. Runs use only the H1 branch, worktree and G1 namespace. CPU Slurm job names begin with `ng-h1-`.

## Cohorts and outcome isolation

- Tier 1 uses GDSC2, CTRPv2 and PRISM20Q2 secondary fitted AUC. Use the corrected 68-parent union, the registered non-core expansions and nonduplicate restored PRISM parents. Three-screen exact common support is 24 parents at any density and four at 80% density. The requested dense cross-screen panel of 40+ is infeasible with these records. The prespecified fallback is three separate fixed 68-drug panels at ≥80% per-drug lineage coverage, prioritizing corrected core drugs then coverage and parent key. The macro average across screens summarizes those same-screen estimates. Its interval resamples independent lineage IDs jointly across screens.
- Tier 2 uses LIMORE native Activity Area on the 50 independently registered lineages and 68 parents. Tier 3 uses oriented LICOB S6 AUC on 43 adult PDOs aggregated arithmetically within 38 donors and 76 parents. More-sensitive values are larger throughout. Tissue and drug-group labels come from registered metadata. The existing HCC TKI group is Sorafenib, Lenvatinib, Regorafenib and Cabozantinib.
- Tier 4 uses the 53 exact matched LIMORE parents and evaluates all 38 LICOB donors. Choice of source uses outcome-free coverage. Target inputs are donor identities, fixed compound identities and measured anchors only. Source slopes and means cannot use LICOB outcomes. Report ranking and correlations in the target's native endpoint and omit absolute transfer errors across the native endpoints. Wong is auxiliary only with ≥15 measured Q entries and at least 23 shared eligible parents. Yang's five drugs cannot meet this rule.
- Immutable source hashes are in the support receipt. The registration process materializes each outer fold's development responses and a separate held label file. Tuning and final fit read the development responses only. Outer splits use one joint hash order for Tier 1 cell families shared across screens, and independently salted sorted sample IDs for LIMORE and LICOB. There are three inner folds within each outer-fit block. Each inner-fit recomputes anchor policies, normalization, RNA OOF, local covariance and eligible 2D prior estimates. Tier 1 cross-study priors use only IDs in that run's fit block. Tier 2 and Tier 3 priors may use eligible 2D reference lineages from the registered public screens, excluding target identities and every study's held local responses.
- A3 and A5 are strict prefixes of A8. Every arm at every K uses the same fold Q after the full A8 exclusion. Assess only measured entries. A sample must have a complete A8 packet and ≥15 measured Q entries to enter the common comparison, including K=0. Include attrition counts. Tier 4/Wong RNA-dependent arms are ineligible. N1 is a seeded derangement moving entire anchor packets. N2 deranges only the query RNA in the best dual model tested on the same support. N1 and N2 are stress controls, not candidates.

## Inner selection and freeze

1. Round 1 tests sample ridge, Ledoit–Wolf, OAS, and SVD covariance on raw/common and per-drug-standardized scales. Ridge grid is 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, and 10 times median covariance diagonal. SVD ranks are 2, 4, 8, 16, with 2, 4, 6 for LICOB. Evaluate log-det, leverage, chemistry diversity and fixed random seeds 971–973. Since policies alter Q, compare each policy's paired excess against its own baseline and report each random seed plus a three-seed mean. Record correction spread, standardized anchor outliers, and mean absolute error by tissue and drug mechanism before Round 2.
2. Round 2 evaluates 2D empirical and chemistry/target shrinkage (alpha 0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75), Huber/winsor/rank transforms, RBF and cosine ridge, and masked bilinear ALS. Include RNA OOF residual covariance and RNA/anchor-kernel blending with RNA weight 0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5 where RNA is allowed. Retain the selected Round 1 policy and the winning linear configuration, plus B4 as the simple fallback. Fit thresholds for robust clipping inside each split. Compare candidates at K=5 on the same Q. The structure baseline uses the registered separable RNA–structure kernels and observed-entry solver, refit for H1 with RNA as the only sample-level covariate.
3. Round 3 refines only the strongest Round 2 families and retains a complex configuration over kNN only with average inner NDCG gain ≥0.002, positive gain in at least two of three splits, and mean precision difference ≥−0.02. B4 tunes neighbors over {3, 5, 10}; B2/B3 select mean or RNA base by inner validation. Inner selection separately fixes the strongest baseline for the primary comparison. H1-Anchor-Best is one named nested selection procedure with fold-local settings. No outer fold's candidate statistics are pooled for another fold's selection. Report H1-RNA+Anchor-Best only if it clears the same inner gain/stability rule against the anchor-only selection. Use its inner winner for N2 even if dual-model promotion fails.
4. Write code and model hash receipts before enabling evaluation. Freeze all models, A8 and Q, and test feature inputs before the held response files become available to the scorer. It predicts from K anchors and only then evaluates against Q outcomes. A single atomic claim prevents repeat scoring. Bootstrap 2,000 paired independent-lineage/donor draws, stratified by study folds, with metrics recomputed where needed. Reuse registered rank relevances and tie-aware expected top-five overlap. Report within-sample Spearman, NDCG@5, Precision@5, within-drug macro Spearman/Pearson and double-centered interaction PCC/Spearman, plus MAE/RMSE for assay-matched tiers. Report pan-cancer tissue, HCC TKI and mechanism data and the five-anchor assay-well and atlas costs. Test single/batch parity and CPU TSV replay at max absolute difference <1e-6 with missing or out-of-range anchor rejection.

## Decision rule

K=5 must improve NDCG@5 by ≥0.02 over the inner-selected strongest simple baseline with paired lower 95% >0 in Tier 1 macro and/or LICOB. Its paired precision lower bound must be ≥−0.02. NDCG@5 lower bounds versus K=0 and permuted anchor packets must both be >0. Report GO-DEV, NO-GO, INCONCLUSIVE or INFEASIBLE. If kNN independently beats RNA-only and scalar, an operational kNN recommendation is allowed even when a complex model fails. LIMORE, LICOB and Wong results are exposed-cohort technical evaluations.
