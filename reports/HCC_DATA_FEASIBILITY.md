# Human HCC perturbation RNA feasibility

Decision: `HCC_DATA_BLOCKED` for this study's native single-cell gene perturbation benchmark. This is the result of the bounded three-candidate audit, not a claim that no suitable dataset exists anywhere. The study requires identifiable genetic intervention, real post-treatment single-cell RNA and controls, with enough distinct interventions for held-out intervention testing. GEO Series and sample records were checked in text/SOFT form (`targ=self&form=text&view=quick` and `targ=all&form=text&view=full`; HTTP 200 for the three series).

| Candidate | Actual intervention and output | Availability and limitation | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| [GSE282326](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE282326), [DDX41 paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-62743-5) | Human HUH7, negative control and two siDDX41 samples, both targeting the same gene; separate large CRISPR fitness screen | GEO lists `GSE282326_RNAseq_HUH7_siDDX41_vs_NC.xlsx`. The file download redirect returned HTTP 403 from the current network. The sample titles and design establish that the RNA comparison is bulk whole-transcriptome profiling for one gene, not per-cell responses for the genes in the screen. | Insufficient RNA intervention identities and no single-cell response labels. |
| [GSE94509](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE94509), [NCAPG paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6662966/) | Genome-wide CRISPR dropout in human HCCLM3 and SNU449, time zero vs. endpoint | GEO lists `GSE94509_Count.txt.gz`. These counts track guide abundance for growth, not post-perturbation RNA. Other cohorts in the study have tumor RNA but do not match screen interventions. | No transcriptome response truth for the screen. |
| [GSE192771](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE192771) | Human Huh7 bulk RNA: MT1G overexpression vs. vector, and sorafenib vs. DMSO; each has three GEO-labeled samples per arm | GEO lists separate expression matrices and normalized read-count files. The arms encode one genetic target and one drug; ATAC samples are a separate assay. No evidence here that the sample labels establish independent donors. | Neither one genetic intervention nor a separate one-drug contrast can support the prescribed held-out single-cell experiment. |

Norman is a K562 benchmark. Human HCC validation has not been run. The separately proposed [GSE264667](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE264667) would be optional HepG2 work with past data exposure and a fresh split; it would be reported as liver-derived exploration, not adult HCC validation or zero-shot transfer.
