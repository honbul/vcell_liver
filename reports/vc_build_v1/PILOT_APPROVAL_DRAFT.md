# VCB-06 pilot design for approval

**Status — design only.** The local laboratory must confirm availability, culture and assay protocols, permitted compounds, and actual price quotes before execution. No experiment has been ordered or run.

## Roster and endpoint

| Slot | Candidate source and role | Evidence required before lock |
|---|---|---|
| DEV01, DEV02 | Candidate authenticated HCC lines JHH-5 and JHH-7, development roles | Current stocks, identity, stability, baseline RNA, growth and assay qualification. Public prior-response exposure is expected. |
| BLIND01, BLIND02 | Two independent adult HCC models with baseline RNA and no response-label exposure in training | Local inventory and STR or unique donor/lineage ID, linked alias/patient exclusion ledger, and independent target embargo. If exposed, label the evaluation as a new assay/site. |
| P01–P24 | Provisional fixed diverse single-agent slate in the [candidate manifest](pilot/provisional_compound_slate.tsv) | Exact assay identities, structural and target domains, authorization, measured dosing metadata and approval. |
| P01–P12 | State subset of the same slate | The planned P01–P12 have exact chemistry support in both public JHH state sources. Confirm relevance, availability and response QC before lock. |

Each drug has the same five functional concentrations across models. Select two of those levels for the 12 RNA drugs. F2 and F4 are planning slots. Signed concentrations require the laboratory's approved solubility and assay configuration. Response at 72 h is a relative assay signal. An independent cell-count readout and the separate predose count can support a narrower interpretation and a future growth-rate analysis.

## Layout and handoff

`pilot_layout.tsv` is a deterministic planning manifest generated by `vc_build.pilot_layout`. It defines 4 model slots × 3 independent culture days and distinct sister-culture plates. A condition moves to a different well across repeats. The 384-well functional design provides 120 treatment, 12 vehicle, 8 qualified positive control and 8 blank slots per model-day. Separate RNA batches contain 24 treated and two vehicle samples per model-day, plus one predose RNA sample. The matched identifier ties 288 conditions across distinct RNA and function wells. Predose count has its own plate. Prepare development and blind models on balanced dates and sequence across batches while restricting access by outcome role.

The laboratory will set assay QC for control separation, biological replicate agreement, readout dynamic range, RNA/library failures and reruns before the manifest is signed. Each rerun must have its own row and reason. Independent date is the replicate unit within a model. Two blind models support a conditional pilot estimate.

Ask the lab and user to approve the actual model IDs, concentrations, assay SOPs, budget, and names of the independent data custodian and scorer. A signed manifest fixes the candidate pool, controls, endpoint, QC and distinct forecast/score roles. Mask target results and allow only predose RNA as inference input. Before handing results to the scorer, freeze forecasts, controls, top-5 rankings, uncertainty and abstentions for the strongest comparator and enabled candidate. The planned primary pilot metric is mean Precision@5 across the two blind models, with a pooled 50% assay-signal reduction criterion that must be finalized for the real assay before opening outcomes. A prospective equal-cost V2 design remains separate.
