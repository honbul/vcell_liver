# VCB-02 and VCB-03 state protocol

## Assay and roles

The CMap 2020 Level 3 Beta L1000 arm uses previously exposed public development wells. Use the hash-pinned R18-excluded target, donor and vehicle manifests documented in reports/r19_plan/data/hcc_lincs24/audit/receipt.json. Inherit the R18 physical and ID exclusions, raw instance QC, exact measured 10 µM and 24 h. Adult HCC targets are JHH-5 and JHH-7. The six nonhepatic auxiliary contexts are A375, A549, HT29, MCF7, PC3 and U2OS. Tahoe has one independently identified adult HCC cell line, SNU-423. HepG2/C3A is hepatoblastoma-derived and SK-HEP-1 is endothelial. Tahoe can inform within-line development. HUH7 Level 3 target plates with protected controls are excluded.

Retain isomeric identity for labels and common drug matching. Use standardized connectivity for clustered comparisons and split grouping. Use independently measured, default unstranded RNA from DepMap Public 25Q3 checked against RRID and sequencing ID. This log2(TPM+1) expression is a basal proxy. Actual matched 0 h is unavailable. Query basal RNA is an input. Query treated and 24 h vehicle expression are scoring outcomes only.

The output is the 24 h drug-versus-vehicle change on 978 measured landmarks, in Level 3 units. Subtract the mean of the registered DMSO on the same RNA plate and cell from a treated well. Prediction reads permitted training wells and controls. Separate scoring reads held wells and their controls after verifying prediction hashes. Eight fit-only PCA axes are derived from unique train context × exact drug conditions. The fit scaler defines unit-variance programme scores. The axes are data-derived. Report original landmark RMSE to retain observability beyond the selected axis.

## Evaluation and decisions

- Both C development slices hold out one adult HCC line. Train on the other adult and the six auxiliary cells. Test exact molecules represented in fit. Predictions in each slice cannot access any treated or vehicle outcomes of its query cell.
- C-both tests the crossed cell difference on precise structures measured at the same dose in both adult HCC lines. Train exclusively on six nonhepatic donors. Respect assay/dose throughout. Conditions are adult-JHH lines × the exact shared compound in the allowed scope.
- Models are B0 zero change, equal-cell exact-drug fit mean, structural nearest neighbour, equal-cell drug mean with shrinkage 0.5, additive basal-RNA residual, ECFP × basal low-rank ridge interaction, and a deterministic fit-only basal swap control. All models receive the same data scope. Distinct plates are averaged within cell and dose, then each line receives equal weight. Hyperparameters and support rules are fixed in the implementation. Measure macro programme RMSE, component directional agreement, landmark RMSE and crossed line-difference errors. Group paired CIs by compound connectivity and report them as conditional on the observed lines.
- Evaluate descriptive repeats only between different physical RNA plates and different well positions for the same line and exact isomeric molecule. Report support. The release does not establish independent cultures for these pairs.
- Advance state only if relative macro programme error falls by ≥5% versus the strongest fair simple model, the paired 95% interval excludes zero in the improvement direction, direction agrees with the gain and ≥10 independent treatment parent-condition repeats per adult context support a repeat check. Advance context only if assay- and plate-independent identification is supported and the crossed-cell error is better than both the common and additive controls. The JHH-7 exact-dose release has a single plate and therefore cannot meet the plate-independent requirement. Results are technical, with separate numerical support and gate verdict.
- The GSE284321 HepaRG arm, if run, is a separate structure+dose model using previously exposed public count-derived contrasts, exact 10 µM at 24 h with held plate and held connectivity. No matched 0 h RNA is available. The model is liver-like and has no HCC or cell-specific interpretation.

The VCB-04 state auxiliary export stores previously exposed, eligible development labels as row-aligned raw contrasts with hashes. Its consumer must restrict cell groups to the training side of the functional fold before any feature fit, auxiliary training or model selection. The auxiliary export is separate from public inference.
