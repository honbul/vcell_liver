# Public organoid source readiness

This is a metadata review for a possible future 3D assay/site evaluation. The VCB-06 slot assignment uses the separately audited LIMORE and adult CMap development resources. No organoid response or individual-level sequence data entered that assignment.

| Candidate | Evidence found | Readiness |
|---|---|---|
| LICOB / OEP003191 | The study reports 65 liver cancer organoids and a 76-drug panel. Publisher tables S5–S8 provide drug catalogue, AUC, IC50 and Emax. The paper points to NODE for original and processed RNA matrices. The project landing page was reachable as a web-app shell in the public spot check. File-level permissions and the exact adult HCC, basal RNA, drug and sample join are unverified. | Candidate for a separately approved 3D data-access and endpoint audit. The author's MTA statement applies to physical organoid distribution. Data read permission must be established against the archive files. [Original article and source links](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10949980/) [NODE record](https://www.biosino.org/node/project/detail/OEP003191) |
| Zhu et al. / HRA004348 | The 64-line bank contains several hepatobiliary histologies and the paper reports profiling RNA in 53 lines. The 301-compound initial screen and reported 64 compounds across 21 selected organoids for detailed curves are separate. The GSA record is linked to PRJCA016100 and public run metadata confirm distinct organoid and biospecimen assays. The exact adult HCC RNA–curve overlap, processed matrix release and file-level access remain unverified. | Candidate for a bounded join of the publisher drug data and authorized processed basal RNA. Sequence access is governed separately by GSA. Multiple runs or assay types for one individual remain one lineage. [Original study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2023.101375) [GSA search record](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/search/specific?db=hra&q=HRA004348) [GSA access rules](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/gsa-human/browse/) |

A 3D analysis would require an accession-to-organoid linkage, exact compound mappings, patient-level grouping, and an assay-specific endpoint contract before scoring. The reported bank totals alone do not establish a covered or unbiased HCC cohort for the 24-compound slate or resolve transfer from 2D cell lines.
