# VCB-06 provisional compound panel

This selection assigns published LIMORE catalogue compounds to 24 planning slots. It uses physical assay identity and development support to propose a balanced panel for the crossed RNA/function experiment. Local model identification, concentrations, product qualification, quotations and execution await lab approval.

1. Read the signed public-external roster as identity metadata. Remove all 50 confirmation lineage families, including related cell lines, before reading selected LIMORE endpoint rows. No scores, forecasts, fitted full-LIMORE packages or expression outcomes from those 50 families are used. Remaining response support is strictly development.
2. For each eligible exact compound, average 72-hour LIMORE Activity Area within each remaining lineage and take the median across lineages. Equal-sized rank thirds define high, middle and low prior response strata. These are relative strata for this public catalogue and its tested protocols. They do not specify local absolute response or a local hit threshold.
3. Match published adult HCC 24-hour CMap state **measurement availability** from the previously audited, unsealed JHH-5/JHH-7 metadata by fully standardized InChIKey. No CMap expression values or forecast scores enter selection. Within each stratum, fill four RNA planning slots by state-context count first, followed by category balance and maximum dissimilarity. This provides 12 RNA slots interleaved across the three strata.
4. Cross-fit a five-nearest-basal-RNA model and a drug mean within the remaining LIMORE development lineages. Exclude the query lineage from every fit and use query RNA with structure. For each cell and compound, compare the two predicted percentile ranks. Average absolute rank differences across cells within each lineage, then across lineages. Fill three functional-only slots using one high-disagreement compound per response stratum. This indicates selection disagreements worth measuring, independently of their local validity.
5. Fill two functional-only audit slots by a seeded choice among the eight most structurally/category-diverse eligible intermediate and weak candidates. Fill remaining slots by structural and category diversity. Dissimilarity is one minus maximum radius-2 chiral 256-bit ECFP Tanimoto similarity to selected compounds, with a small bonus for a less represented published target category.

Every stratum contributes eight compounds, of which four are eligible for the RNA planning subset P01–P12. The panel accepts at most one compound per connectivity group, uses distinct exact product IDs, and joins with the frozen plate slot layout. The file records the published catalogue codes, not a stock or safety claim. Confirm the designated targeted RNA conditions in independent local development before a blinded pilot. A planned concentration is never derived simply from the public activity-area rank.
