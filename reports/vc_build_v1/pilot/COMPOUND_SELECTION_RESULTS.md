# VCB-06 provisional compound assignment

The candidate slate has 24 actual LIMORE catalogue compounds, with eight each from the high, middle and low development Activity Area strata. P01–P12 supply four compounds per stratum for matched 24-hour RNA and 72-hour function. All 12 match documented adult JHH-5 and JHH-7 24-hour state measurement availability by exact structure. That metadata establishes historical assay coverage. Local response magnitude and reproducibility await development measurement.

The 24 exact identities span 24 parent-connectivity groups and 24 scaffold groups, nine publisher target categories and 16 publisher pathway categories. In the nonconfirmatory development comparison, rank-disagreement choices span mean absolute model rank shifts of 13.6, 17.0 and 18.5 percentile points for bortezomib, CB-839 and erastin respectively. These are pairwise model ordering differences, conditional on the published catalogue and the authorized development lineages.

The response summaries use 2,288 measured outcomes in 26 models from 24 independent development lineage groups. All 50 planned external confirmation lineage groups were excluded before endpoint selection. The exclusion also removes a related line outside the 50 representative query IDs. Cross-fitting for rank disagreement uses only the remaining basal RNA and activity area. The pilot roster, its lineage exclusion evidence, exact compound identities and SHA-256 receipts are available with the layout.

The slate is provisional for laboratory approval. The five functional concentration slots and two matched RNA slots remain symbolic. Current inventory, assay qualification, final concentrations and local model identities still require laboratory confirmation.
