# Repeat-weighted PRISM labels for source-held and LICOB transfer

Use all 24 exact fully specified LICOB compounds for which the corrected register contains a single-agent PRISM20Q2 product. This support is determined from structure and protocol metadata. LICOB responses have previous exposure and the comparison is technical. Start with prior LICOB fit rows, add any missing registered PRISM cell–compound rows for those parents, retain a single registered protocol for each row, and use the same fit cells and compounds for all arms. [Prior chemical registration](LICOB_COLD_RESULTS.md) · [Prior dose failure](VINORELBINE_RAW_DOSE_RESULTS.md)

## Labels and model

- Verify the 20Q2 raw normalized log₂ fold-change and well metadata. Require single-agent identity and STR-matched rows, at least six planned doses, and at least two finite independent detection plate identifiers per dose in a complete source curve. Take the two detection readouts in lexical order for repeat agreement, and take the median of all finite readouts per dose for the consensus. A cell must have a complete consensus and repeat profile on the registered dose axis. The threshold is at least 100 complete cells per compound overall and at least 30 fit groups for each fold dose statistic. Cells failing QC at a selected compound are removed from every arm at the same support. Detection plates measure technical reproducibility. [Public assay release](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.20564034.v1) · [Study processing](https://github.com/broadinstitute/repurposing/blob/master/depmap_reprocessing_script_for_secondary.R)
- Compute 0–1 clipped viability from fold change. For each compound calculate normalized trapezoid weights on the measured log-dose axis. The first raw target uses those weights. The candidate multiplies those same weights by `0.05 + max(0, rho)^2`, where rho is Spearman between detection readouts across fit lineages at the dose. For a constant replicate axis use rho zero. Renormalize weights to sum to one. The third active arm is the publisher's fitted AUC on the same rows.
- Use the prior GDSC-anchored positive affine calibration on the exact common fit observations and preserve its compound means and non-PRISM residuals across arms. Within each compound's selected PRISM source rows, center negative raw targets and match the published PRISM residual standard deviation, with denominator floor .02 in the native fraction and final standardized amplitude ratio clipped to [.25, 4] relative to the PRISM affine slope. All scaling and reliability estimates use fit data only. Same equal-assay per-drug contributions and fixed structural kernel (RNA, pathway, TF, Chronos/genomic proxies, ECFP, targets), penalty 4. Do not select hyperparameters or vary architecture.

## Evaluation and gates

- Source held-out development uses the same three lineage groups from GroupKFold on the existing training roster and holds the entire evaluation assay out of fit. Evaluate CTRPv2 and GDSC2 separately on registered candidate compounds in both fit and evaluation. Minimum 5 compounds with at least 30 evaluation lineages overall. Report the same observations across the three rules and the compound-mean control.
- LICOB primary is the eligible exact-PRISM panel, with all 43 HCC organoids and the 38 previously verified adult donors. Freeze predictions without reading S6. The primary candidate must improve both within-drug correlations for both active baselines (fitted AUC and raw equal-log-dose) with paired 95% lower bounds >0. Within-donor Spearman, NDCG@5 and Precision@5 must each have paired lower bound >−.005 versus both. Apply the same rule separately to each 2D held assay. Report technical adult-HCC-only source support and pharmacological strata. The 2D and LICOB evaluations have separate gates.
