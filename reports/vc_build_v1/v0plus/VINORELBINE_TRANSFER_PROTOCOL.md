# Vinorelbine across source screens and replicated HCC PDX cohorts

The prediction target is the ordering of cell or patient-derived models under a single exact compound. The primary external task uses the common Singapore HCC PDX patient groups with treatment-naive RNA and both 2023 and 2024 published vinorelbine 3 mg/kg T/C values. Those publications reuse PDX lines. Prior work audited their response agreement and selected this technical task. No other external labels are used to select settings. LICOB and the registered Wong prediction panels lack this exact compound. [PDX data and exposure](SHUEN_PDX_RESULTS.md)

## Source model

- Use PRISM20Q2 exact parent `GBABOYUKABKIAF-IELIFDKJSA-N` from the corrected catalogue and GDSC 8.5 native single-agent 140 and 2048. Require the official native label and tartrate synonym for both and stable Sanger model plus COSMIC identifiers. Carrier salt is stripped at registration. Use the prevailing physical AUC protocol per assay selected by cell coverage. Set sensitivity to minus native AUC and standardize within each training assay. Equalize assay weight, then estimate one shared response residual across GDSC1, GDSC2, and PRISM. [Official GDSC endpoints](https://depmap.sanger.ac.uk/documentation/datasets/drug-sensitivity/) · [NCI compound and assay cross-reference](https://discover.nci.nih.gov/drugs/cellminercdb/Vinorelbine_cellminercdb.html)
- Outer source development simultaneously withholds one assay and held lineage groups from all three screens in three GroupKFold splits. Choose L2 penalty on the mean of held-assay pooled-lineage Spearman using only these 2D data. Candidates are `.05, .5, 4, 16` for dual kernels and `1, 16, 256, 4096` for feature Ridge. Pooled comparisons are drug mean, RNA/pathway Ridge, RNA-only kernel, full RNA/pathway/TF/Chronos/genomic proxy kernel, and the primary full kernel with 25% weight on a fixed mechanism kernel. That kernel uses rank-normalized `TUBB, TUBB3, ABCB1, ABCC10, DLGAP5, FBXW7, RB1, NF2`. Terms are estimated from source data and have no manually enforced effect direction. Additional controls are GDSC2-only and PRISM-only with the full kernel and the selected pooled full penalty. [Vinca-site interaction](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-53139-y) · [ABCB1 and knockout sensitizers](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29689640/) · [ABCC10](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19082471/) · [TUBB3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2816659/)
- Pathway/TF blocks, feature scaling, imputation, and genomic/Chronos RNA proxies are fit on the applicable source lineage set. No unsupervised fitting on PDX. Score raw count CPM as primary and GENCODE v36 length-based approximation as sensitivity under the previously registered RNA contract. Source summary reports between-screen AUC correlations on the exact same cell and on the shared adult HCC cells.

## Freeze and test

Seal every method's predictions on the 17 RNA profiles before a separate job reads the published response tables. Apply the registered conservative patient grouping. Report correlations and paired patient bootstrap intervals separately for each endpoint on the 2023–2024 common group and each publication's full RNA support. The common group must include at least six patient groups. The primary CPM candidate must have positive 95% lower bounds for both Spearman and Pearson versus drug mean, Ridge, and full kernel in both publications on the same group. Both publications are evaluated on the same people and are not independent replications of model generalization. Drug ranking across compounds and results on LICOB or clinical patients are outside this test.
