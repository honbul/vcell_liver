# Hold LICOB predictions fixed outside seven restored PRISM measurements

This technical evaluation uses the previously exposed LICOB 43 HCC organoid profiles, 38 verified adult donors, and 76 AUC endpoints. The historical cold-model fit and frozen predictions are the reference. The response workbook is read only by the scoring stage. The exact-parent registration confirms zero unspecified stereocenters for each restored drug and supplies 3,676 single-agent PRISM observations. [Historical cold assessment](LICOB_COLD_RESULTS.md) · [PRISM correction](SHUEN_PDX_RESULTS.md)

- The only added rows are PRISM for DZNeP, dovitinib, FG-4592, GSK1838705A, ispinesib, PF-562271 and ponatinib. Previously supported gemcitabine is outside this intervention. Registration hashes, data coverage, the train lineage set, target annotations, and the positive affine endpoint calibrations are held fixed. Fit the existing `interaction_transfer` at its registered penalty. No setting or scale is selected on LICOB.
- Three comparators and one primary prediction use the exact same 76×43 roster. Historical chemical mean and historical RNA interaction are taken from the verified forecast. The primary arm uses the historical mean plus the new RNA residual for the restored seven compounds and historical RNA interaction for all others. The direct arm uses both the new source mean and the new residual for those same seven. This isolates the contribution of predicted donor differences from the change in drug means. Raw newly fitted values for other drugs are excluded before freezing.
- Score the seven-drug common panel as primary, and full 76-drug support as secondary with the previously used donor grouping. Compare the primary arm with historical interaction and historical mean. A positive lower paired 95% bound for both within-drug correlations versus each comparator is required. Within-donor Spearman, NDCG@5 and Precision@5 must each have a lower paired 95% bound above −.005 for both comparisons on the same seven drugs. Report per-drug differences and all five metrics for the direct arm. The biological endpoint is published AUC, whose concentration ranges differ between PRISM and LICOB.
