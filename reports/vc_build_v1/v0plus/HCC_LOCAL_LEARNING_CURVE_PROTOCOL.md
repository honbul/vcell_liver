# Fixed local supervision study on the LIMORE and LICOB drug panels

This same-study development diagnostic uses previously evaluated public cohorts. It measures whether increasing assay-matched supervision improves a held-donor prediction. The original scores for independent GDSC2·CTRPv2·PRISM transfer, the external superiority criteria and the offline release are unchanged. LIMORE is the primary public 2D external cohort independent of access to OEP003191 or HRA004348. Its external 50-family identity audit and measured mutation/CNA comparisons retain their independent evaluation records. [LIMORE registration](../data_function/VCB01_PUBLIC_CROSS_SUPPORT.md) · [LICOB 76-drug registration](LICOB_COLD_RESULTS.md)

- Use all 50 LIMORE adult HCC families and 68 drugs, and all assay-linked adult LICOB donors and 76 drugs. The fixed LIMORE response is Activity Area. The LICOB response is negative S6 AUC. Order unique families by SHA-256 of `491881:{study}:{family}` and assign every third family to the same outer fold. Train with either the first 8 available families or all other families in that ordering. Held identities and the full drug panel are shared between budgets. All organoids from a donor remain in one fold. Average sibling organoids' kernel rows and training observations within each donor and average the sealed predictions within each scored donor.
- Keep the registered archival forecasts byte-locked. LIMORE references are the corrected structured transfer, drug mean, Ridge, CellHit, CODE-AE and Velodrome. LICOB references are the 76-drug interaction transfer, drug mean and nearest chemical response transfer. The original transfer predictor supplies the source score scale and contrast. RNA features, Hallmark and TF activations and RNA-derived Chronos dependency features are fitted on the corrected GDSC2·CTRPv2·PRISM RNA axis. The fixed chemistry kernel uses exact compound identity, ECFP Tanimoto and mapped drug targets. Use recipient RNA only to compute cross-kernel values. No recipient-wide fit of normalization or dimensionality reduction.
- Compute the local mean and the local mean shrunk toward four pseudo-donors from the source mean. The positive affine map from source compound means uses the fit compound mean IQR ratio clipped to 0.25–4 and the median residual. Center the source within each drug over the fit donors and multiply by the median fit-drug IQR ratio clipped to 0.25–4. This gives the calibrated source contrast. Normalize fit residuals by each drug's target IQR with a floor of 0.25 times the median fit-drug IQR and a 1e-6 floor on that median. Remove the drug and donor mean from the normalized residual. Fit the centered RNA × drug kernel at penalty 4, restore each drug's scale and add the correction with fixed weight 0.5. The comparison arms are the plain and shrunken local mean, shrunken mean plus calibrated source contrast, the shrunken mean plus a kernel fitted directly on local two-way contrasts, and the main arm with fit donor residuals permuted using a single fixed cycle derived from seed 43386.
- Score within-drug Spearman and Pearson across held families **within each fold** and within-family drug ranking by Spearman, NDCG@5 and expected top-five overlap. The full-data arm passes only if, in both cohorts and against each comparison on identical rows, the paired 95% lower bounds exceed zero on both within-drug correlations and −0.005 on all three drug ranking metrics. The full-budget versus 8-family comparison uses the same bounds independently. Correlation intervals use 1,000 paired fold-stratified family×drug bootstrap draws. Ranking uses the existing 4,000-replicate paired family bootstrap. Report HCC TKI, kinase, EGFR, HDAC and cytotoxic strata where supported.
- A preparation job signs a separate fit-only label table per development fold. Each G1 GPU task receives only its own fit table plus outcome-free query inputs and writes an independently hashed freeze. A separate scorer checks the three freeze hashes and common identity support before reading the registered response file. Retain per-donor data and full forecasts on G1. This diagnostic does not promote a model or reopen the external acceptance decision.
