# Calibrate RNA rank context separately for patient and drug ordering

On the same gene list, reranking expression within the pancreatic PDO intersection raised LICOB NDCG@5 and lowered the within-drug Spearman point estimate. The earlier evaluation attributed part of the change in forecasts to compound-specific offsets. Test whether source-only calibration of the two contexts can retain patient ranking and improve drug prioritization. [Motivating technical result](RNA_GENE_PANEL_RESULTS.md)

- Fix the previously registered native-context forecast P and restricted-context forecast R, with identical RNA gene membership and their original structured kernel, source calibration, and anchor. Use their frozen predictions and the same frozen structured, Ridge, drug mean, CellHit, CODE-AE, Velodrome and V0+ companions. Pin both RNA-context score file hashes and verify each forecast hash. Keep the same three grouped folds within each held-source condition, three pooled folds, LIMORE 50×68 and LICOB 43×53 with the 38 adult donor aggregation. Both external cohorts have prior evaluation exposure.
- In each outer fit, assign lineages to two balanced folds by sorting SHA-256 of `10492:<group>` and alternating. Refit both rank contexts inside each of the two partitions with the same molecular and auxiliary inputs. Calibrate using the resulting inner held forecasts and their source outcomes. Retain only inner validation drugs observed in their inner fit. Outer validation lineages, outer excluded assay outcomes, and all LIMORE and LICOB outcomes are outside calibration. In the two external fits, use the documented platform-specific available RNA columns and the same source-only calibration; the drug offset can differ across platforms because the rank context can differ.
- For each core compound, estimate `d` as the inner held mean of `R−P`, averaging repeated observations within each lineage first and then giving each lineage equal weight. Use zero if the compound has no inner support. The primary prediction is `P+w(R−P)+(a−w)d`. It combines two patient-level scores and applies one shared coefficient to the drug-specific offset. Once the source settings are locked, the primary model can operate on one sample at a time.
- Choose `w` from 0, .25, .50, .75, 1. For every assay, average its within-drug Spearman and Pearson separately over the drugs and the eligible inner folds. Require at least five lineages for each correlation. Among nonzero candidates whose improvement over P is strictly positive in both metrics for every fit assay, maximize the minimum improvement over these metrics and assays. Tie-break with smaller `w`. Use 0 if no positive candidate qualifies.
- With `w` fixed, choose `a` from 0, .5, 1 and `w`. On the inner validation forecasts use samples with six or more measured drugs, count lineages equally within each assay, and count assays equally. Maximize mean NDCG@5 among settings with per-assay changes in within-sample Spearman and Precision@5 at least −.005 relative to `a=w`. Break ties by proximity to `w`, then by smaller `a`.
- Compare native P, reranked R, fixed equal mixture, selected mixture without separable offset (`a=w`), native P with the full source offset (`w=0,a=1)), and a transductive diagnostic that uses the mean prediction difference within the unlabeled query cohort as the offset instead (`w=0,a=1`). Neither offset-only arm may use query outcomes. The transductive arm tests whether the drug-average change in the observed platform is transferable; it is separate from the primary decision. Pure drug-specific intercept shifts preserve within-drug rankings and correlations within one component.
- Fit both rank contexts on G1 and record each inner split, offset list, and fixed selection trace. Freeze the calibration receipt and forecasts before the separate scorers read evaluation outcomes. Require paired 95% lower bounds above zero for both within-drug correlations against the established published and structured/Ridge/drug-mean models and the reranked model. Require lower bounds above −.005 for these correlations against P and the equal mixture, to verify retention of patient-level prediction. Against each of those references, require all three within-sample ranking bounds above −.005, and specifically require NDCG@5 improvement with its lower bound above zero against P. Apply the joint gate to at least two held assays and both external cohorts. Use 800 crossed resamples for source and 1,000 for external within-drug intervals. Show paired controls for source and query offsets and compare each mechanism group descriptively.
