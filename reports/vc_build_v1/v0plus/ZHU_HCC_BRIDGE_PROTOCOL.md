# Public HCC organoids as a development bridge for LICOB and LIMORE

The public Zhu HCC cohort has completed its original fixed external score. Its development role in this run is explicit. LICOB and LIMORE retain their fixed target manifests and are previously scored technical recipients. Zhu's published sampling site is Eastern Hepatobiliary Surgery Hospital. LICOB reports Zhongshan Hospital. Public identifiers do not establish patient-level linkage between these studies. Apply the joint gate in both the whole and directly supported panels and in both recipients. Resample drug×donor blocks 2,000 times for correlations and use the existing 4,000-lineage bootstrap for ranking. Preserve the two original external decisions and offline release.

## Unit correction before recipient results

Zhu's 10 µM table stores inhibition in percentage points. Convert those observations to fractions before applying the fixed positive affine mapping, whose slope bounds assume fractional responses. The signed development table retains the original numeric units. The initial freeze used unconverted numbers. Its fit-only residual audit caught the slope hitting the lower bound and a residual RMS of 4.61. The dependent scorer had started and was canceled before publishing any score. No result from it entered this correction. The original panel, model and comparison thresholds are retained.
