# Paired RNA assays for cross-platform cell features

Test whether independent Affymetrix and RNA-seq measurements of the same published CCLE cell model help preserve drug-response information when transferring to a different assay or HCC population. The earlier RNA common-axis comparison moved both external point estimates. This protocol separates gene support, rank-PCA feature extraction, and pairing. [Earlier culture constraint results](CULTURE_CONSISTENCY_RESULTS.md) · [CCLE expression release GSE36133](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE36133)

- Register GEO GSE36133 series-matrix RMA data and the exact DepMap 25Q3 Model catalogue. Link GEO cell-line titles to an unambiguous normalized CellLineName or StrippedCellLineName. Exclude multiple samples mapping to the same model. Use Entrez identifier probe sets, a unique approved protein-coding HGNC symbol, and existing baseline RNA support. GEO supplies published across-array RMA. The model learns all transformations on its own fit partition. Keep both measurements on the training side of every matched pair. Source validation holds out whole lineage groups and assays according to the existing 12 registered splits. [Approved symbol source](https://www.genenames.org/download/archive/)
- For each fit, re-rank RNA within each sample on the common gene axis. In matched fit pairs with tissue groups of at least five, standardize each platform separately with a .05 floor and clip at 8. Remove tissue mean to define phenotype scores on the array. Take the top 64 principal coordinates of the tissue-centered, standardized matrix. Use ridge prediction of those coordinates from fit-pair RNA using a normalized linear RNA Gram and fixed penalty .30. Use each query RNA alone to compute that feature. Preserve array-score rather than coefficient variance when estimating geometry. Tissue labels of query cells are unnecessary.
- The matched controls are the native structure model on that same intersection, an identically computed 64-dimensional target using the sequencing RNA instead of Affymetrix, and cyclically assigned array profiles within each fit tissue with seed 51957. A secondary control reweights RNA genes by the square root of .25 + .75 times the square of the positive paired within-tissue correlation. For all four added feature conditions, use .65 of the common-axis native structure cell kernel and .35 of a trace-normalized added linear/RBF kernel. Keep RNA pathway and TF terms, inferred Chronos and genomic features, chemical kernel, drug anchors, and drug-response regularization 4.
- Benchmark the same matched rows of LIMORE 50 × 68 and LICOB 43 × 53, and group LICOB predictions by the 38 recorded adult donors at scoring. Include frozen full-axis structure, drug mean, Ridge, CellHit, CODE-AE, Velodrome and V0+ on those IDs. Both external cohorts have previous evaluation exposure. No external outcomes, cross-query averages, or held RNA pairing examples enter feature fitting.
- Freeze each prediction component with SHA-256 before accessing the scores. Primary is paired-array features. Compare both within-drug correlations with paired donor/lineage × drug intervals, 800 resamples per source fold and 1,000 in external. Require Spearman and Pearson lower bounds above 0 versus each registered comparator and within-sample Spearman, NDCG@5 and Precision@5 lower bounds above −.005. Promotion requires at least two of three held screening assays and both external cohorts. Report grouped mechanism metrics externally, assay supports, effect sizes and input hashes. Existing full-panel HCC verdicts remain part of the study history.
