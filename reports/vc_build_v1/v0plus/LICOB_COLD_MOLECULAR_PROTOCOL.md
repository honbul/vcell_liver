# Drug-ID-free molecular transfer across compounds and HCC donors

The fixed structured model for LICOB scores all 76 drugs. Nineteen have no exact parent in the source fit. Eighteen of those have fully specified stereochemistry. The earlier cold-transfer estimate on that 18-drug panel missed the joint gate. The previous molecular neural network is indexed by trained drug ID. This experiment extends the molecular network for new chemical parents and tests whether its predictions add complementary personalized signal to the fixed structural forecast. [Full LICOB panel](LICOB_COLD_RESULTS.md) · [Existing molecular and public comparisons](STEP3_MOLECULAR_CV.md)

- Use the same registered GDSC2·CTRPv2·PRISM training release as the full 76-drug model, with 670,830 measurements over 1,186 parents. Train within each of the three previously sealed joint scaffold and lineage splits. Use the original exact-parent identities and exclude any same-connectivity and same-scaffold fitting compounds for source evaluation. Check each restored validation key against the archived baseline before fitting. For external prediction fit once on the full registered source panel and use the same network for both cohorts. The external cohorts are the locked 50 adult LIMORE lineages and 43 HCC PDOs from 38 adults in LICOB. Compare the original 68 and 76 drugs respectively. The cohorts have prior evaluation exposure. The evaluation is a technical comparison of new frozen forecasts on those external populations.
- Retain the V0+ architecture's rank-normalized high-dispersion RNA, PROGENy/Hallmark/DoRothEA features, Chronos and HCC driver auxiliary supervision, continuous target-conditioned attention and expression/chemistry gating. The chemical input is ECFP 2,048 plus four RDKit descriptors. The new models omit trainable drug IDs for every compound. The RNA feature transform and target gene vocabulary use only the fitting cells and fitting catalogue. Held pharmacological target annotations can activate genes in the fit vocabulary. Scoring inputs supply their own RNA and molecular descriptors only. Alignment uses the existing inductive source CORAL objective. The matched ECFP/RNA ablation disables pathway input, target attention, Chronos supervision and genomic supervision, while preserving the shared selected gene panel and source alignment. It estimates their joint contribution.
- Align source endpoints using exact common fit cell–drug pairs. Fit aligned assay-and-drug residuals with the existing smooth L1 and paired residual loss for 45 epochs, width 192, batch 4,096 and existing seed 11711. For each query drug subtract the average predicted residual over fit-cell RNA before adding the locked anchor. This applies to both standalone models. No query batch statistics or responses enter the centering. Use the archived chemical-mean anchor for source and LICOB and the archived drug mean for LIMORE. The primary arm mixes the existing structural model and the full neural model equally. Also score the equally weighted structural plus ablated neural model. Report exact support, no-exact-parent support, fully specified no-exact-parent support, and registered drug mechanisms. Comparators on source and LICOB include the archived chemical mean, neighbor and interaction forecasts and the ablation blend. On LIMORE they include the structural model, drug mean, Ridge, ElasticNet, LightGBM, CatBoost, CellHit, CODE-AE, Velodrome, the existing V0+ molecular model and the ablation blend.
- On identical rows the primary must have paired 95% lower bounds above zero for both within-drug Pearson and Spearman and above −.005 for within-donor Spearman, NDCG@5 and Precision@5 versus every designated comparator. The joint gate applies to at least two held source assays and both external cohorts. LICOB must meet the gate both on the full 76 drugs and the 18 fully specified no-exact-parent drugs. Score native assay results by holdout fold, bootstrap source 800 times, external 1,600 times and use the existing 4,000-lineage draws for within-profile metrics. Report the other arms on all supports. Each comparison remains on its registered rows and the settings stay fixed through external scoring. No model promotion by subgroup choice.
- Run G1 GPU prediction and freeze with archived comparisons verified at their original byte hashes. Invoke the separate score jobs after the complete GPU array exits successfully. Check every forecast hash and parent linkage against its score receipt. Keep patient-level inputs, models and forecasts on G1. The current releases retain their version identifiers pending the joint decision.
