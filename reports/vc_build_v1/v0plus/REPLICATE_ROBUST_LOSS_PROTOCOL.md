# Assay-robust HCC response learning from exact repeated cell models

The corrected structural kernel aligns endpoint scales and combines the loss from different screens. If a drug response predictor fits one screen's cell ranking at the expense of a second screen's, the pooled loss may obscure that difference. Use exact matched training cells to test an assay-robust loss while fixing the molecular inputs and anchor. This is a change to the training objective. [Corrected source alignment](STRUCTURED_CV_PROTOCOL.md) · [Earlier response rank and agreement weighting](ASSAY_CONSENSUS_PROTOCOL.md)

- Keep the registered single-agent GDSC2, CTRPv2, and PRISM20Q2 core, common anchors and corrected source-specific residuals, and existing weight per observation. Use the same 12 lineage and nested screen-holdout splits as the verified off-panel run. Match comparator forecasts against that run's approved SHA-256 and exact row identities. Use the existing external references for LIMORE 50×68 and LICOB 43×53 with adult donor aggregation to 38. Both external datasets have prior evaluation exposure.
- For each fitted drug, select the maximum number of assays with at least 35 identical cell models observed in all selected assays. Within that number choose the largest overlap, breaking ties by sorted source/endpoint order. Collapse multiple observations of each exact source/cell/drug to a median. Require each chosen assay in the set to have mean squared residual at least `1e-4` and standard deviation at least `.005`. If the chosen set fails support or energy, try the next smaller assay count. The matched loss is used only on these fit cells, including the case where only two of the three fitted assays are eligible.
- For a supported drug `d`, reduce the weight of the original squared losses by 30%. Use that 30% allocation for the matched losses. For each supported assay divide its matched mean squared error by the mean squared error of its zero-residual prediction, with a floor of `1e-4`. Multiply the combined normalized risks by the original total weight of the drug and the mean zero-residual mean square across its matched assays. The main `replicate_robust` condition combines the normalized risks using `τ log(mean(exp(risk/τ)))`, with `τ=.10`. An unsupported drug keeps the original contribution to the shared objective.
- The same-support `replicate_balanced` condition uses arithmetic mean instead of log-mean-exp. The `replicate_shuffled_assays` condition retains the robust loss and independently shuffles the assay labels within each exact cell-drug replicate set using seed 37193, including renewed baseline error scales. The main observation loss in all conditions stays intact. The matched targets are not replaced by independent cell or drug permutations.
- Use the original product kernel, with RNA rank features, PROGENy/Hallmark/DoRothEA, inferred Chronos and driver features, and ECFP/target drug geometry, and RKHS penalty 4. Whiten the positive spectral directions retaining eigenvalues above `1e-7` times the maximum. Start from the standard solution. Verify that its query predictions agree with the full original kernel within `4e-5`. Solve the loss divided by total original weight using GPU L-BFGS with at most 180 iterations, strong Wolfe line search and history 15. A nonfinite or increased objective or final maximum absolute gradient above `5e-5` aborts the component. Restore the fixed anchor to each prediction. Record fitted overlap, baseline errors, objective change, convergence and the mean largest soft assay weight. Implement prediction with RNA and registered drug inputs alone.
- Freeze all components, then run source and external scoring separately from fitting. The primary candidate must achieve a positive paired 95% lower bound for both within-drug Spearman and Pearson versus the structured kernel, matched balanced, matched shuffled, drug Ridge, and drug mean. Require the paired lower bounds for within-sample Spearman, NDCG@5, and Precision@5 to exceed `−.005`. Apply the same rule against CellHit, CODE-AE, Velodrome and V0+ externally. Require two of three held screens and both external cohorts to pass. Use 800 source and 1,000 external crossed lineage and drug resamples for the two within-drug measures. Pooled metrics, mechanism strata and the external common-sensitivity and interaction decomposition are descriptive. Preprocessing and loss estimation use training observations only.
