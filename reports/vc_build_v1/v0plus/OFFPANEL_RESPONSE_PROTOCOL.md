# Off-panel pharmacology supervision for HCC RNA features

Learn how to represent baseline RNA from measured cellular sensitivity to hundreds of other single agents. The earlier expansion fed additional responses directly to the chemical kernel and did not improve held-screen sensitivity. Here the auxiliary responses shape a cell kernel, while predictions for each evaluated drug retain the corrected core response, pharmacological features, and anchor. [Direct expansion benchmark](EXPANDED_PANEL_CV_RESULTS.md)

## Registered inputs and fitting

- Use the SHA-256-verified CTRPv2 and PRISM 20Q2 expansion and conservative stereo-checked GDSC2 expansion. Restrict to the original corrected core's fit cell IDs and sources in each split. Exclude all InChIKey connectivity groups in the corrected 68-drug panel and query. Verify the lineage assignment for each retained row and disjointness from held lineages. There is no external response or query-cohort statistic in fitting. Source validation uses the same twelve joint assay–lineage or pooled lineage splits.
- Treat source × endpoint × compound as a separate supervised task. Orient its observations to higher sensitivity and take a median for repeated fit cells. A task needs at least 50 cells and nonconstant measured response. Transform each task's own fit observations with clipped normal scores (average rank, .5 offset, ±3). These scores supply an auxiliary cell representation. The existing compound anchors, positive source alignment, and target drug responses retain their corrected native scheme.
- The primary representation subtracts the task mean within each fit tissue, retaining observations only if there are at least eight in that task and tissue. Preserve the observed mask in every fit. For each task with at least 20 usable rows, fit a ridge map (penalty 8) from the leading 128 fit-only eigenfeatures of the existing rank-RNA kernel. Multiply each task's fitted signal by the square root of mean task count per source divided by that source's count. Extract the leading 64 directions of the centered fitted multivariate response. Use the same composition on query RNA alone, through the training eigenbasis. Mix the resulting trace-normalized half linear, half radial cell kernel at .25 with the full structured cell kernel at .75. Keep the structured kernel's PROGENy, Hallmark, DoRothEA, inferred Chronos and driver values, ECFP/target drug kernel, and core penalty 4.
- Compare the same model and weights with directly computed top 64 RNA spectral features, the centered observation mask, whole auxiliary profiles permuted within fit tissue (seed 40721), and off-panel supervision with global scores before tissue-centering. The observation-mask model predicts which measurements exist from RNA. Permuting whole profiles keeps within-tissue associations among tasks and their missingness while breaking individual response links. Keep frozen corrected structured, drug-specific Ridge and drug-mean comparators in all splits. Include existing CellHit, CODE-AE, Velodrome and V0+ on the same external rows.

## Evaluation

- Freeze predictions and hash every component before running a separate scorer. GPU execution produces the 12 source files and the LIMORE and LICOB files. The external comparisons use the established 50 LIMORE adult lineages × 68 drugs and 43 LICOB PDOs × 53 drugs (38 adult donor groups at scoring). These cohorts have prior evaluation exposure. Record fit supports by assay, compound and cell, plus clinical TKI, targeted/kinase, and nonselective cytotoxic strata.
- Require both within-drug Spearman and Pearson paired 95% lower bounds above 0 versus each registered comparator (800 source and 1,000 external crossed lineage/donor and drug resamples). Require within-sample Spearman, NDCG@5 and Precision@5 lower bounds above −.005 in the same comparisons. Advancement requires two of three held-out source screens and both external cohorts to pass. Pooled is a sensitivity analysis. A positive result here would enter subsequent independent confirmation rather than replace an existing held-out score.

On the current registered splits, rebuild the three source references with the same corrected structure kernel, registered per-drug Ridge setting (penalty 1,000, 512 genes, scale 1), and corrected anchor. The source forecast roster, supervision, and decision rule stay as registered. The two external components retain their original sealed forecasts and historical references.
