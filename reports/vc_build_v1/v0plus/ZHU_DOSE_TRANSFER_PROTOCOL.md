# Zhu paired assay audit and 10 µM matched supervision

Zhu's first frozen forecast was shared across the dose-curve and 10 µM endpoints. The two previously reported panels differed in drugs and patients as well as measurement. This technical follow-up fixes the same 16 adult HCC donors and 18 exact compounds so that assay concordance can be measured on all 288 common pairs. Both response sheets have prior project exposure. [Original freeze and score](ZHU_HCC_CONFIRMATION_RESULTS.md) · [Public study methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10897507/)

- In the common grid compare the measured negative 1-AA and the measured 10 µM inhibition ratio. Use cross-donor Spearman and Pearson per compound, drug and patient marginal correlation, centered donor×drug interaction correlation and reciprocal patient ranking. Use 2,400 crossed donor and drug resamples for across-donor and interaction intervals. Keep per-drug estimates. Score the same frozen predictions against both responses on those exact pairs. This is cross-assay agreement on a follow-up selected panel. It cannot identify repeat reliability or a measurement noise ceiling.
- Candidate training uses the fixed GDSC2·CTRPv2·PRISM20Q2 single-agent rows for the 37 exact Zhu primary-screen parents. Reuse the prior RNA axis, construct all feature fits on 2D training cells, and use the same published source releases and chemical IDs as the first freeze. Extract viability at an administered dose within 0.5% of 10 µM, or interpolate in log concentration between adjacent administered points with a ratio ≤4.001. Reuse CTRPv2 post-QC curves and SNP matches, source GDSC2 blank/DMSO normalization with Z′≥.3 and verified dose window, and the PRISM provider fit after matching published AUC to .005 and confirming the administered range spans 10 µM. PRISM uses that continuous fit within its dose range. No extrapolation. Clip viability to 0–1 and aggregate duplicate curve summaries, never response rows from the query. [Existing source extraction contract](LICOB_DOSE_ENDPOINT_PROTOCOL.md) · [GDSC raw QC](GDSC_RAW_WINDOW_PROTOCOL.md)
- Require at least 30 source cells per exact drug and at least 12 supported drugs to fit. Keep all 21 registered Zhu donors on eligible drugs. Use the existing two-way centered RNA–pathway–target–Chronos kernel and fixed penalty from the LICOB endpoint run. Compare `matched_endpoint` with `matched_auc` on identical source observations, after orienting native source AUC by its declared direction. Both models use a common 10 µM drug mean plus the fitted interaction. The drug mean alone, frozen full-source structure, per-drug Ridge, CellHit, and the frozen ICC-offset drug mean and CODE-AE are additional same-query-support comparators. All new fits are inductive. Keep full support for the original published scores separately.
- Candidate passes when all comparisons have crossed 95% lower bounds above zero on both within-drug correlations and donor-bootstrap lower bounds above −.005 for all three within-donor ranking metrics. The near-term question is whether direct dose alignment changes transfer on the same training and test identities. No score from a previously observed cohort can by itself promote the released model or establish both LIMORE and LICOB superiority.
- Register the source rules and code first. Write and verify source receipts, freeze assay replay and any candidate forecasts together, then open and match the original S4 workbook in the dependent scoring job. Store small audit results in Git and retain source tables and predictions on G1.
