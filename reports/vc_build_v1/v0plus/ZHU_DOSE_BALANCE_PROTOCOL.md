# Dose supervision with matched training scale and prediction amplitude

The previous 10 µM and native AUC models used a common drug intercept. Their external interaction RMS differed by a factor of seven. This technical benchmark isolates the difference in source supervision while controlling its scale and the influence of the predicted interaction. It also adds assay-and-lineage holdouts on exactly the same paired cell-line measurements. [First paired Zhu assay and dose analysis](ZHU_DOSE_TRANSFER_RESULTS.md) · [Public cross-study dose harmonization](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8769697/)

- Reuse the prior freeze and source-extraction hashes for exactly 22,824 rows with both labels and the same 32 evaluated drug parents. Fit the same RNA, pathway, drug-target and Chronos kernel and penalty to both targets. Orient both labels as larger-is-more-sensitive. Within each training source and compound subtract the response mean and divide by its IQR. Floor each IQR at one quarter of that source's median nonzero drug IQR and an absolute minimum of 1e-6. Use the same weighting and remove sample and drug main effects by the existing observed-grid weighted projection.
- Build three interaction forecasts from 10 µM supervision, native AUC supervision, and their equal mixture. Use one drug intercept equal to the equally weighted mean source sensitivity at 10 µM. Set each model's interaction RMS within each compound to the fitted 10 µM RMS multiplied by that drug's median physical IQR across training sources. Compute RMS on fit cells with the fit observation weights. The blend averages the two amplitude-matched predictions, then applies the same positive RMS calibration. Reuse the fixed Zhu RNA axis of the original experiment and estimate each transform only on the relevant training rows. In particular, holdout RNA never estimates output scales.
- For source comparisons, make three GroupKFold lineage partitions of the paired observations. In each partition fit on the two other screening assays' training lineages and predict the held assay's held lineages. Evaluate parents with 30 training lineages across admitted sources and five held lineages in that fold. Proceed only when each source/fold has two parents and 20 assessed lineages. Evaluate both truth definitions on the same held rows. The 10 µM endpoint determines source gates and AUC is descriptive.
- The external technical panel remains the already scored Zhu 21 donors × 32 drugs at 10 µM. Give all methods the same 672 observations. Report the existing unbalanced AUC and 10 µM, full-source structure, per-drug Ridge, CellHit and the two fixed ICC-offset comparators as recorded. Report drug target and drug-family strata using the published annotations and the prior classification rules.
- For dose versus balanced AUC and 10 µM mean, require the lower 95% bounds above zero for within-drug Spearman and Pearson, and above −.005 for all three within-specimen ranking metrics. The blend also compares with the balanced dose model. On Zhu require the same criteria against every frozen reference as well. Resample lineages and drugs jointly 2,400 times for the first two metrics, preserving source folds, and use the existing 4,000 paired lineage draws for ranking. A joint result requires at least two eligible source passes and the Zhu comparison gate. Zhu's prior exposure limits the interpretation of any pass. The LIMORE and LICOB promotion criteria stand.
- Freeze the complete supported predictions before the dependent source and Zhu scoring job. Store forecasts on G1 and aggregate receipts here.
