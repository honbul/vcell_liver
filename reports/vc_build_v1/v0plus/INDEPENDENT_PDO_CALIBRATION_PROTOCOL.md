# Independent HCC organoid reference for RNA rank calibration

The earlier technical experiment separated a common drug offset from an individual change in predicted response. The LICOB query-averaged offset preserved within-drug correlations and reproduced most of the increase in NDCG@5. A 2D fit-only offset did not transfer. This experiment estimates the offset on independent HCC organoid RNA and separately learns which drugs benefit from changing RNA rank context. [Motivating results](RANK_CONTEXT_CALIBRATION_RESULTS.md)

- Fix the corrected 2D GDSC2/CTRPv2/PRISM core, the same 12 outer splits, and the same 50 LIMORE lineages and 43 LICOB models. Score LICOB at the adult 38-donor level. Both target cohorts have prior evaluation exposure. Keep the same frozen source and external public-model references. Neither target cohort provides treatment labels or batch statistics for the primary estimate. The target platform's registered input gene list is known at deployment.
- In both contexts keep the same genes, namely the outer available RNA axis intersected with Wong, Yang and the pancreatic PDO gene list. In P rank first on the outer available RNA axis intersected with Wong and Yang. In R rank only on the retained gene list. Keep the molecular, pathway, TF, target, ECFP, Chronos/CNV/driver architecture and penalty 4. Refit both contexts at each outer split and each of its two fit-only inner lineage folds. The archived wider-axis P and R stay as comparison arms.
- Within each drug, estimate the change versus P for a convex blend of P and R, with R weight 0, .25 or .5. An eligible assay needs at least 30 distinct validated lineages in both inner folds and all correlations defined. Average within assay across the two folds. Require at least two eligible assays. Both Spearman and Pearson must improve by at least .002 in every eligible assay. Maximize the smallest improvement and break ties toward the smaller weight. Default to zero. This weight affects within-drug individual ordering.
- On LICOB use the average of two independent study centers. Compute mean R minus P per drug on previously registered Wong adult HCC 10 donors and Yang adult HCC 74 donors, first averaging multiple PDOs within donor. Yang P1 and P2 remain excluded. Compute the per-study means from 2D-trained models on the expression only, with no reference treatment outcomes. Cohort accession and study-specific ID checks do not establish cross-study patient identity. For the held 2D assays and LIMORE use the corrected 2D inner OOF lineage-average change instead of the 3D center. Hold the center constant across patients.
- The primary prediction is P + w(drug) × (R − P − center) + center. Isolate the drug-specific mixture and the fixed offset and score P and R on the same gene axis. External technical controls include the two separate study offsets and the held cohort's own unlabeled predicted mean shift. The latter is transductive. Report the agreement of independent offsets with this diagnostic and how many drug weights move off zero.
- Freeze calibration receipts and predictions before separate scoring. Against matched P, archived P/R, the fixed offset control and the existing structured/Ridge/drug mean and public external benchmarks, require paired 95% lower bounds above 0 for both within-drug correlations and above −.005 for all three within-sample metrics. Require a strictly positive NDCG@5 lower bound versus matched P on LICOB. Apply the joint gate to at least two held assays and both external cohorts. Use 800 paired resamples for source and 1,000 externally, the existing compound strata, and exact matched roster. The variant advances only if it clears these gates.

The published RNA and registrations are already on G1. No additional responses enter training. [Wong published study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12205652/) · [Yang RNA registration](YANG_HCC_REGISTRATION_PROTOCOL.md) · [Existing reference model](STRUCTURED_CV_PROTOCOL.md)
