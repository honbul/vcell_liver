# Matched 2D/3D RNA as a prediction consistency constraint

A viability model trained in monolayer can respond to large culture-dependent changes in RNA that do not carry the same drug-response information in spheroids. Test a finite consistency penalty that shrinks prediction changes along directly measured, same-line 2D-to-3D contrasts. The hypothesis is shrinkage of a nuisance component, not exact invariance of drug sensitivity. The design uses an independent HCC cell-line culture experiment and no paired or unpaired RNA from the external query population to fit the penalty.

- Use GEO GSE298810 processed FPKM for Huh7 and HLF in monolayer, 3D inject-embed and 3D mix-embed, two biological replicates per line and condition. Require the audited 12 columns and their hash. Use unique protein-coding gene symbols common to the registered expression axis. Fit the native structured RNA, pathway, TF and auxiliary scalers on 2D fit cells only. The RNA model is otherwise the existing corrected separable kernel with the original chemical/target kernel, assay anchors, weights, and penalty 4. [GEO GSE298810](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE298810) · [Published culture experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13077891/)
- For each line and each 3D format, use one 3D-minus-2D mean prediction contrast, each mean over its two replicates. With bridge evaluation operator D, covariance K, and each row of D normalized to unit RKHS variance, condition K on four zero contrasts with independent unit noise. This gives K conditioned = K − K Dᵀ (D K Dᵀ + I)⁻¹ D K. It yields a positive semidefinite kernel and permits nonzero effects. The bridge scores are predictions evaluated on external cell-line RNA, without bridge drug response labels. Report the fraction of training kernel trace removed.
- Refit three controls on the same gene axis. No conditioning, conditioning on four differences between replicate 1 and 2 of the same 2D or inject-embed condition, and conditioning on four contrasts after swapping Huh7 and HLF 3D culture identities. All contrast rows use the same variance normalization. Use GSE142206 Huh7 Matrigel strata and GSE263674 HepG2/C3A spheroid time series only as descriptive RNA direction checks. The latter is a liver cancer cell-line derivative, kept separate from adult HCC.
- In three existing lineage folds per held source (GDSC2, CTRPv2, PRISM) and the pooled sensitivity, remove Huh7 and HLF donor groups (DepMap ACH-000480 and ACH-000393) from both fit and evaluation to isolate additional RNA exposure. The four models and drug mean use the same restricted outcomes. In LIMORE and LICOB external technical comparisons these two cell lines may appear in source training, and the external registered query IDs differ from those lines. Retain all rows of LIMORE 50 × 68 and the established LICOB 43 PDO × 53 exact supported drugs, then score the 38 adult donors after grouping. Include the archived full-axis structure kernel, drug mean, drug Ridge, CellHit, CODE-AE, Velodrome and V0+ on those same external IDs.
- Lock model settings and fit before unsealing this run's labels. Freeze each source fold and external forecast with SHA-256, then run independent scorers. A gate requires positive paired 95% lower limits for within-drug Spearman and Pearson and lower limits above −.005 for within-sample across-drug Spearman, NDCG@5 and Precision@5, relative to each registered control in that comparison. Source advancement requires at least two of the three held assays. External advancement requires both previously evaluated external cohorts. Report point estimates, paired ranges, and HCC TKI, targeted and cytotoxic strata. This additional 53-drug technical comparison leaves the separate full LICOB 76-drug validation and original study verdict intact.
