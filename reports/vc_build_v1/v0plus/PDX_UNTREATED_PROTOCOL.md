# Untreated-adjusted PDX response as an intermediate pharmacology domain

Gao and colleagues released baseline RNA and longitudinal tumour volumes for an in vivo PDX screen. Assess whether adding pharmacological response measured outside the 2D cell line domain preserves donor differences in a wholly held cancer tissue. PDX response is a distinct assay from PDO viability. This benchmark validates an intermediate training source before a further HCC comparison. [Original study and Supplementary Table 1](https://www.nature.com/articles/nm.3954)

- Use the published FPKM, raw PCT and curve metadata sheets. Keep BRCA, CRC, NSCLC, PDAC and cutaneous melanoma. Use model ID as the unit. The workbook does not identify every biological donor across model IDs. Hold the entire primary tissue in each test. The untreated label is the explicit same-model reference, even though the PCT metadata classifies it as single. Require an unambiguous positive tumour-volume series with day 0 and observations reaching at least day 14 in both arms. Reject multiple observations at a day without a replicate ID. Use linear interpolation on log volume to estimate log fold growth at day 14. The primary endpoint is untreated minus treated log growth. The negative treated growth alone is the unadjusted control. A day-14 matched endpoint may select against early termination. Report support on that endpoint and do not generalize to excluded curves.
- Register the named small-molecule treatment codes with a unique PubChem isomeric parent in the fixed registry. Retain gemcitabine-50mpk as the only gemcitabine regimen and the main binimetinib regimen. Exclude the separate low-dose binimetinib label, Abraxane formulation, biologic antibodies, combinations and unresolved labels. A study name or sponsor code links the released identity. Experimental lot stereochemistry is not available in the workbook. For 2D training use the existing expanded, exact-parent registered GDSC2, CTRPv2 and PRISM20Q2 cell-line responses with their baseline RNA. In 2D use the exact parent when at least 100 lineages support it. Otherwise use at most three of the structurally closest 2D fit parents with ECFP4 Tanimoto at least .25 and support at least 100 lineages. Average those predictions using similarity weights. If none qualify, the 2D residual is zero. Publish exact and analogue support. [Chemical responses from PubChem](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) · [2D input registration](LICOB_COLD_PROTOCOL.md)
- A tested drug needs at least five held PDXs, at least 20 fit PDXs outside that tissue and at least one fit tissue with five PDXs. Within the fit source and drug, standardize midranks of the oriented 2D endpoints. Within each fit PDX tissue and drug, separately standardize the unadjusted and untreated-adjusted sensitivity midranks. Fit the existing rank-RNA, high-variance gene and pathway/TF mixed linear/RBF kernel with ridge penalty 4. Fit scalers and genes in the corresponding response training domain. No held RNA cohort statistics enter these fits. Use the same drug-specific mean and standard deviation of adjusted responses from the other PDX tissues to anchor and scale all methods.
- Compare drug mean, 2D-only, PDX unadjusted, PDX adjusted, the fixed equal mixture of 2D and unadjusted PDX, and the primary equal mixture of 2D and adjusted PDX. Freeze the six matched forecasts for each held tissue and score in a separate process using held raw and adjusted endpoints. Report within-drug Spearman/Pearson and within-PDX across-drug Spearman, NDCG@5 and Precision@5. For the primary ranking gate restrict to PDXs with at least six drugs to make Precision@5 meaningful. Report native-vs-adjusted observed rank agreement and a technical analysis on exact 2D-supported drugs.
- A tissue passes if the adjusted mixture has both within-drug 95% paired lower bounds above 0 versus drug mean, 2D-only and the unadjusted mixture. Resample PDX and drug blocks in 800 draws. Require at least ten ranking-eligible PDXs and paired lower bounds above −.005 for all three ranking metrics against those references. Advancement to a separately registered HCC benchmark requires three of five tissue passes. Neither LIMORE nor LICOB response values participate in this experiment. Preserve their earlier benchmark and full goal.
